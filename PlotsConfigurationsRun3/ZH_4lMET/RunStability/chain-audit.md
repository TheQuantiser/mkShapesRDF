# 2024 lower-pT RunStability chain audit: Coffea versus mkShapesRDF

**Scope and revision.** This audit concerns only the four 2024 HLT-path AND selected-Z-flavor categories: Ele30→ee, Ele23–Ele12→ee, IsoMu24→μμ, and Mu17–Mu8→μμ. It excludes Mu50, Ele115, eµ paths, inclusive categories, tag-and-probe fits, and high-pT probe inputs. Coffea `main` was read at `2b5ba43` and mkShapesRDF `ZH_devel` at `c064927` on 2026-09-30. The completed Coffea v5 `nominal/` histograms use `matched_z_v2`; the default `2024-stability-only.yaml` still uses `matched_z_v1`. Historical mkShapes plots used a compiled campaign and HWWNano produced from an unavailable dirty worktree. Current source describes the intended/current chain, while the retained files and compiled historical selection establish what the published campaign actually consumed. See the [mkShapesRDF HWWNano issues report][hww-issues], especially its distinction between producer defects and RunStability consequences.

## The question and the answer

The useful comparison has three parts: (1) do the four named categories share the intended offline working points, HLT paths, event-quality and luminosity concepts; (2) does Coffea reproduce the **historically executed** HWWNano→RunStability event population and weights; and (3) do the HWWNano association errors enter Coffea's independent NanoAOD→histogram path?

**Answer:** the 2024 v5 mode reproduces several intended ingredients, including the four HLT bits, the selected-lepton HLT-safe and final working points, strict 35/35 GeV and 60–120 GeV cuts, common MET flags, low-pT HLT-safe horn cleaning, and the selected-pair low-trigger OR weighting convention. It **does not reproduce the historical selection or input population exactly**. The most material deliberate differences are the omitted leading-two tight gate, different DATA stream domain, trigger-object-associated category-specific pair selection, and direct central-NanoAOD processing rather than the HWWNano MC skim. The observed historical tight-bit and electron coordinate association errors are avoided by Coffea's per-object NanoAOD records and original object indices; this is a code-path assessment, not a claim that every Coffea output has been independently audited event by event. The published full-year differences must therefore not be interpreted as a simple MC scale or luminosity failure.

The historical HWWNano used the same four lower paths; an aggregate trigger OR adds no independent acceptance once one of these concrete bits is required. The mkShapes `L2TightLeading2` gate is **not** a requirement to import into the desired Coffea analysis merely because it is in the old compiled campaign.

## MC weights, exposure, and output

The old MC producer stored `baseW` from its full-source `Runs.genEventSumw` and its upstream skim; the downstream selected event applied generator weight, MET flag, pileup, selected-pair lepton SF and selected-pair low-trigger OR SF. Coffea v5 records uncut `Events.genWeight` in every physics partition, fills signed raw weights with source multiplier, pileup and selected-pair lepton and low-trigger OR factors, and applies `1000 × xs(pb) × L_source(fb⁻¹) / sum(uncut Events.genWeight)` once at a **complete** source merge. It rejects gaps and does not turn a partial source into an absolute prediction. [mk aliases][mk-aliases] · [Coffea weights][c-processor] · [source finalization][c-finalize].

The v5 `nominal/` low path uses the **historical low-trigger OR convention evaluated on its selected pair**. That factor is not itself a calibrated efficiency correction for each separately matched HLT path. The v5 `nominal_uncorrected/` view omits it. The `reference_hlt_bit/` diagnostic uses global Z arbitration and the HLT bit without selected-pair matching; it is useful for separating pair-policy effects but still has Coffea's source tier, ownership and omitted production gate. The default `matched_z_v1` instead has no selected-pair low-trigger SF, requires its computed production gate, uses its broad-lepton horn policy, and has its own older normalization contract. It is **not** the completed v5 campaign; do not silently use its configuration to explain the v5 published yields. [v1 config][c-v1-config] · [v2 config][c-v2-config] · [Coffea view selection][c-processor] · [selection guide][c-selections].

Both published period projections sum selected DATA run bins and project the full-year source MC template with recorded **path-period exposure / that source's reference luminosity**. The 28 matched category-period exposure values agree and the mass plots share 60 visible 1-GeV bins with zero flows. The earlier compiled mkShapes reference luminosity (109.08 fb⁻¹) and v5 source reference (109.72830897472497 fb⁻¹) cancel their respective upstream MC factors in this projection; their difference is not an extra 0.6% MC correction. The current mkShapes configuration is a later snapshot and must not be confused with the compiled historical reference. [Period comparison][c-period] · [mk projection][mk-plot] · [Coffea projection][c-plot].

The revised [mkShapesRDF issue report][hww-issues] explicitly **rules out an earlier allegation** about the nominal `JetSelMask` masked-count/unmasked-coordinate expression for the active 2024 sequence: lepton pT is descending, filtering preserves order, and jet cleaning precedes lepton correction. The scenario “a below-10-GeV retained lepton precedes an above-10-GeV retained lepton” does not arise there. Its separate conditional JES variation jet-index concern has no demonstrated nominal effect. Coffea `docs/selections.md` still repeats the nominal-jet accusation; that paragraph needs correction. The two independent jet implementations also require eventwise comparison before numerical equivalence can be claimed.

## What remains missing for the user's goal

1. **Choose the target explicitly.** For trigger-associated low-pT Z stability, v5's path-specific HLT bit plus object association and selected-pair effective WP implement the requested selection; historical exact-yield reproduction is a different target. Preserve v5's omission of the leading-two gate. If an exact historical comparison is needed, use the separately labeled `reference_hlt_bit/` view and acknowledge that old HWW data and MC can be irreversibly affected.
2. **Do not equate source tiers or ownership.** The 40-component HWW DATA campaign includes MuonEG priority and the historical MC `__l2tight` skim; v5 has 32 EGamma/Muon records and complete central-NanoAOD MC. Equal category names do not imply identical accepted events. Quantifying full-year contributions would require a campaign-wide matched event-key/source audit, not another luminosity rescale.
3. **Do not claim fully calibrated per-path DATA/MC from the low-trigger OR SF.** The copied selected-pair OR weight is a useful common historical convention; a path-associated normalization claim would require a validated correction for the actual HLT-path/object-matching definition, as well as correction and source equivalence where claimed.
4. **Keep producer evidence separate.** Fixing aliases or replotting cannot restore MC events excluded by HWW `l2tight`. The shared HWWNano association findings belong to the mkShapesRDF framework and its affected campaigns, while Coffea's v5 report documents their impact on this personal RunStability comparison. A full-year Coffea result cannot repair historical HWWNano; the original NanoAOD is the regeneration source.

**Conclusion.** Coffea v5 is a coherent *new* low-pT trigger-associated Z RunStability analysis that shares important intended cuts and weighting conventions with mkShapesRDF while avoiding the demonstrated HWW positional-bit error. It is **not a bug-for-bug reproduction of the historical mkShapes result**, and the published differences are explained in the paired prefixes chiefly by historical object association and the upstream MC skim, with additional deliberate routing, gate and pair policies. The exact full-year split among those mechanisms, and numerical equality of independent corrections on every event, remain unestablished.
