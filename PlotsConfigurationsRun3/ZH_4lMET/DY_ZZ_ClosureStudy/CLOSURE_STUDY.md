# DY–ZZ closure study

## Status

The configuration, validation tests, full production, merge, summary
extraction, and plotting are complete.  Campaign
`dyzz_closure_full_fd_20260812` processed all five eras in 6,850 Condor jobs;
every job passed the strict batch check and all five merged ROOT files passed
the structural validation.  The tables and figures cited below were generated
from those fresh full-profile merged files, not copied from the old ZZCR
campaign or inferred from an event-limited pilot.

**Nonprompt/fake background is not included.**

## Questions answered by the final tables

The generated `summary/` tables provide, per era and combined period:

- full and BASE DATA/MC ratios and statistical errors for S0–S8/D0–D2;
- nested-covariance-aware efficiencies and κ values for adjacent transitions;
- N−1 category closure using the variable associated with the removed cut;
- prompt process composition and non-DY-prompt/fake-limitation flags;
- absolute and area-normalized χ²/ndf and maximum bin pull;
- ZEE/ZMM, 4e/4mu/2e2mu, exclusive trigger, and exclusive stream closure;
- extra-lepton closure at S8;
- BASE→lepton-SF→trigger-SF→full correction ablations;
- current-only, eventPt-only, both, and migration fractions for enriched and
  broad DY contracts.

The eight numbered summary figures and compact representative distributions
are generated by `make_plots.py` for each individual era, combined 2022,
combined 2023, 2024, and ALL_RUN3.

## Interpretation rules

No conclusion is drawn from a raw stage ratio without checking κ, prompt
composition, flavor, era, trigger/stream, PU, jet activity, and weight
ablation.  In particular:

- a commissioning discrepancy fixed by the full prompt model is composition,
  not automatically DY physics;
- ZEE-only or ZMM-only behavior points first to flavor-specific corrections;
- one-era behavior points first to production/calibration/trigger/PU/lumi;
- one exclusive trigger/stream points to trigger or de-duplication handling;
- coherent `pT(Z)` and `phiEtaStar` behavior supports recoil/QCD modeling,
  while poor `pT(Z)` with better angular `phiEtaStar` points first to lepton
  momentum scale/resolution;
- a trend across EXTRA0→EXTRA1→EXTRA2P is a topology/composition effect;
- correction-induced shifts are separated from population effects with BASE
  and ablation counters;
- better closure in an alternate pT population is not by itself a reason to
  change the nominal analysis selection.

## Quantitative completion fields

1. **First materially deteriorating cumulative release:** releasing the b veto
   (`S3_NO_XFLAVOR` to `S4_NO_BVETO`) changes the full DATA/MC ratio from
   1.104 to 1.137 and gives kappa = 0.9496 +/- 0.0102.  Top/ttV/tZ rises to
   3.40% of prompt MC at S4.
2. **Largest transition deviation:** `S7_FOURL_BRIDGE` to `S8_Z_BRIDGE` has
   kappa = 1.094 +/- 0.029.  This is a transition from a four-lepton population
   to a high-statistics Z bridge and should not be read as a single-cut scale
   factor.  Among ordinary cumulative releases, the b-veto transition above is
   the largest deviation.
3. **Most consequential N-minus-one removal:** removing the X-mass requirement
   adds 223 DATA events (1003 to 1226), the largest count change, while moving
   DATA/MC slightly toward unity (1.093 to 1.086).  None of the N-minus-one
   ratios identifies a single cut that explains the S0 normalization.
4. **Normalization versus shape:** both are present.  ALL_RUN3 full DATA/MC is
   1.093 +/- 0.040 in S0, 1.154 +/- 0.030 in S7, 0.9655 +/- 0.0001 in enriched
   DY, and 0.9580 +/- 0.0001 in broad DY.  Area-normalized DY shape chi-square
   values remain very large, especially in jets, `pT(Z)`, `phiEtaStar`, PU,
   and MET.  These are diagnostic rankings only because systematics are absent.
5. **Process composition:** S0 is 99.69% ZZ.  S7 is 94.02% ZZ with 3.42%
   top/ttV/tZ, 1.53% VVV, and 0.83% Higgs.  Enriched DY is 99.08% DY; broad DY
   is 97.79% DY with 1.28% Vgamma/Vgamma-star and 0.74% top/ttV/tZ.
6. **Flavor and era localization:** integrated broad ZEE and ZMM ratios agree
   (0.9567 and 0.9588), so the main deficit is not flavor-specific.  It occurs
   in every era: 0.9209, 0.9828, 0.9565, 0.9683, and 0.9548 for 2022, 2022EE,
   2023, 2023BPix, and 2024 respectively.  S0 has only 60--611 events per era,
   and its apparent era spread is correspondingly much less precise.
7. **Trigger and DATA-stream localization:** the dominant Muon and EGamma
   streams agree at 0.9654 and 0.9645.  MuonEG is 1.162 but small.  Exclusive
   double-electron (1.227) and ElMu (1.162) contrast with double-muon (0.941),
   single-electron (0.950), and single-muon (0.966), motivating a targeted
   priority-path efficiency check rather than a de-duplication change.
8. **PU and jet correlations:** in S8, ALL_RUN3 shape chi2/ndf is 17189 for
   `PV_npvsGood` and 8912 for `nJet30`; both vary strongly by era.  PU/jet
   modeling must therefore be checked before assigning the residual solely to
   DY recoil.
9. **`pT(Z)` versus `phiEtaStar`:** both are discrepant after area
   normalization in broad DY (chi2/ndf 2879.6 and 1678.1).  `pT(Z)` is worse,
   but the angular observable is not sufficiently well described to isolate a
   pure momentum-scale explanation.
10. **Extra-lepton topology:** S8 ratios progress from 0.9655 (EXTRA0) to
    0.9307 (EXTRA1) and 1.0633 (EXTRA2P).  The last bin has only 1,954 DATA
    events and the pattern is era-dependent, so it flags topology/composition
    modeling rather than a monotonic universal trend.
11. **Correction impact:** broad DY moves BASE 0.8695 -> selected-lepton
    0.9542 -> selected-lepton+trigger 0.9580; S0 moves 0.8944 -> 1.0723 ->
    1.0723 and then 1.0926 after the full b-veto correction.  Selected-lepton
    corrections dominate; trigger and b-veto corrections are smaller but
    visible in their applicable domains.
12. **pT-contract migration:** broad eventPt-only contains 1,537 DATA versus
    2,274 MC events, DATA/MC = 0.676.  It is only 1.03e-5 of the DATA union
    (MC 1.45e-5); current-only is empty by construction.  Enriched migration
    is even smaller, 4.49e-6 in DATA.
13. **Current DY versus a different population:** enriched and broad ratios are
    0.9655 and 0.9580.  The broader population has more Vgamma/top content and
    a slightly larger deficit, but the residual already exists in the enriched
    population.  It is not caused by the alternate event-tight pT definition.
14. **Next targeted study:** first validate the selected-lepton SF mapping and
    PU/jet inputs per era; then compare DY recoil/generator shapes with full
    uncertainties.  In parallel, check exclusive double-electron and ElMu
    trigger efficiencies.  A four-lepton nonprompt estimate is required before
    interpreting the S4--S7 excess causally.
